# Life Cycle Cost Exercises

Two independent classroom exercises about counting costs nobody bills you for.

## 1. The Campus Fleet (game)
Open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages link). 5 to 10 minutes. Students pick a shuttle fleet, see the bill the university pays, see the unbilled costs and who bears them, then choose how much those count. The winner changes with their assumptions. The last screen makes a short summary to copy and bring to class. Nothing is saved or sent. All dollar figures are made up for teaching.

## 2. Marlowe streetlights (Claude chat)
Same format as the King Davis exercise. Instructor testing steps:
1. Read the scenario slides first.
2. Open claude.ai, choose Sonnet 5.5, use an incognito window, start a new chat.
3. Open `advisor-prompt/marlowe-advisor-prompt.txt`, copy all of it, and paste it into the chat (do not upload the file).
4. Check that the bot replies with: "I'm the senior advisor to Mayor Ortiz."
5. Give a recommendation and talk with it for about 5 minutes. It should ask one question per turn, give no answers, and stay serious.
6. Type SUMMARY. Check that it writes under 150 words with the five headings, including "Costs I counted and costs I left out, and who bears them."
